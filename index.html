Welcome

Welcome

You are invited to join us for the Biennial Convention of the Church of God (Seventh Day) June 28 – July 3, 2027 in Greensboro, North Carolina! Our Convention theme is In His Presence.

From Sabbath gatherings to daily life in this world, we all sense our great need for deeper revelation and increased awareness of God’s presence in our lives. Please plan now to attend this life-changing week of fellowship, learning, and worship as we explore what it means to gather, live, experience, be transformed, minister, and serve in the very presence of God.

Preparing the heart and house for worship, abiding daily in Christ, beholding His glory, hearing His voice, and being sent on mission with Him: By the grace of God, this convention will stir fresh hunger for God’s manifest presence. Come expecting to experience the restoring, revealing, and transforming work of the Holy Spirit as we are brought into the presence of the King!

Yes, come ready to meet with God.

Join us for In His Presence.

Please pray with us as we prepare for this event. May all experience His grace, His joy, His Spirit through His glorious presence among us as we gather in His name!

In His Presence. . . everything changes.

Volunteer

Volunteer

Serve at Convention

Volunteers make convention possible, and there are many areas in which to serve. If you are interested in volunteering, please indicate your area(s) of interest when you register.

If you have additional questions, you may email us at conventioninfo@cog7.org .

Areas of Service

There are many ways you can help make convention possible.

Young Adult Program

Assist young adult (ages 18-25) activities in various ways, including registration, cleanup, media/sound, photography, setup/takedown, and prayer team.

A/V Team

Load and unload equipment (Monday prior and Friday afternoon/Saturday night after convention), assist in helping with tech needs of various rooms, and serve as stage hands.

Transcription

Quickly transcribe proposed edits for projection during business sessions, and/or operate the projection computer.

Children's Program

Be willing to smile, guide kids in their activities, and have fun. Also help with setup, snack time, and small group activities.

Nursery

Child care for small children. If you plan to take advantage of this service, we encourage you to volunteer in this area at least one morning or afternoon.

Translation

Interpret English into Spanish or Spanish into English.

Safety and Usher Team

The safety and usher team volunteers will assist in providing a safe and orderly convention. They will direct attendees to meeting rooms, serve as greeters, collect offerings, and coordinate with the Convention Center security.

Voter Check-in Team

Check in voters for business meetings, distribute and collect voting devices, verify and count voters. Working laptop required.

Youth Program

Work at the registration desk, supervising fun activities, being a helpful hand and watchful eye on our outing, and ministering to youth through prayer and friendship.

Loading / Unloading Equipment

Unloading convention equipment and supplies Saturday night and Sunday before convention. Packing up and loading equipment again on Saturday night after convention.

Medical Team

Assist and coordinate with the Salt Palace Convention Center paramedic to provide first aid.

Photographer

Take photos of workshops, worship services, programs, and more. Volunteers must provide their own equipment.
